## Result

WP-03.90 and the WP-03 section 8 completion gate are **not accepted** by this record. Every offline gate that Contracts runs passed at the verified candidate (section 3), but three findings keep the closure incomplete and one required evidence form was not produced:

1. **Six public `connector.*` operations have no authored service.** Registry04 section 5 specifies `ConnectorService` (ListDefinitions, ListConnections, BeginConnection, CompleteConnection, GetConnection, RevokeConnection). No proto defines it, no delivery-graph task names it, and the operation-scope gate reports exactly these six rows as `pending` (329 registered, 6 pending, 7 reserved). The CON.19 start prerequisite for CON.18 reads "full operation-scope manifest with zero pending rows"; the observed state is six pending rows. A local twin exists: CON.05 (complete) authored `IConnectorBroker` as `ConnectorBrokerService` (six `private-helper` rows, registered), but the `ConnectorDefinition`, `ConnectorConnection`, `ConnectorChallenge` and `ConnectorProof` records exist only in the internal `arcforges.local.platform.v1` package, and a public proto may not import local schema.
2. **Five Registry04 records are absent by name and their owner is undetermined**: `LocalCallContext`, `SimulationDataSegment`, `SimulationSample`, `SimulationEvent` and `SimulationGap` (section 4).
3. **The compatibility harness protects only the ci.113.1 baseline.** The retained published descriptors are `ArcForges.Contracts.Foundation` and `ArcForges.Contracts.PublicApi` at 1.0.0-ci.113.1. The hosted report states that full coverage of the later product services is outstanding (its wording attributes this to "the CON.18 completion prerequisite", which is stale: the CON.17 ledger record is `delivered` and the matrix is the CON.17 completion follow-up). That follow-up is claimed (CON.17 epoch 1, `w-c20261002-con17f`) and its PR, Contracts 76, is open, unmerged and unreviewed as of 2026-10-02.
4. **No consumer restore of the candidate was performed in any ecosystem.** The task outcome asks for isolated consumer restore of the published candidate artifacts. P2-017 forbids installed-package consumer execution in CI, no local diagnostic was run for this task, and no package payload was downloaded. What was observed instead is described in sections 3 and 5.

## 4. Independent Registry04 coverage cross-check

Method (claimant-observed offline reading, not reproduced by another worker): extract every `| id | Service.Method |` row of `docs/architecture/contracts/04-protobuf-wire-registry.md` at Design main `7c8a4cf2456ccc6e8c2bea9e2067694cea9ec082`, and every `service`/`rpc` pair from the authored `.proto` files of this repository at the base commit; extract every record name from the numbered-field tables of the Design `docs/architecture/contracts/*.md` and look for it among identifiers in the authored proto, JSON schema, fixture and tooling files.

- Operations: 297 registry rows. 246 match an authored proto method. 38 are the in-process typed ports (13 scope, 7 chat and 18 infrastructure) that CON.06 exports as static adapters, not gRPC services. 7 are the reserved Hub entries (`IHubRegistry`, `IHubRouting`; `FUTURE.*`), reserved by design. The remaining **6 are `connector.*`**, matching the six pending oracle rows exactly.
- Records: 313 distinct record names. 302 are present. Six absent names belong to the reserved Hub routing entries (`Registration`, `LocalLease`, `HealthReport`, `ResolutionContext`, `CapabilityBinding`, `ContributionFilter`) and are excluded from current registration by design. **Five are absent without a determined owner**: `LocalCallContext` (the `x-af-call-bin` local call metadata of annex 09) and the `af-segment.v1` payload family `SimulationDataSegment`, `SimulationSample`, `SimulationEvent`, `SimulationGap` (architecture 23 says the native reader consumes this published Contracts encoding). No delivery-graph task text assigns authoring of any of the five: the PLT.24 notes only reference `LocalCallContext` as a consumer, and the four Simulation records are named by no task. No message with the `LocalCallContext` field shape exists. Registry04 lines 271 to 274 define the four Simulation records with numbered field tables, and section 10 says `af-segment.v1` serializes `SimulationDataSegment` as canonical UTF-8 JSON ("not arbitrary protobuf encoder output"); architecture 23 says WP51 implements it while the native reader consumes the Contracts-published encoding. So the payload is serialized as canonical JSON, and whether a Contracts proto message is also needed is part of the owner decision; "absent" here means no authored message, profile, schema or vectors under those names.
- Not examined: the provider callback exception `/callbacks/connectors/{providerId}` named in Registry04 has no authored HTTP schema here; whether it needs one was not determined.

Whether the five records and the public `ConnectorService` belong to Contracts, to another task, or are to be retired is a design decision for the Architecture Owner through a planning change. This record does not decide it and does not add or move any task. Options noted by the independent reviewer, not decisions: for `ConnectorService` and its public records (which also need a placement decision, since the local records cannot be imported), a new Contracts closure task, or an amendment of CON.05 or CON.13, with a Cloud runtime owner named separately (none exists), or retiring the six public rows from Registry04 section 5, catalogue 00 and manifest 11; for `LocalCallContext`, amending CON.05 (annex 09 is already its authority) or retiring it; for the four Simulation records, amending CON.21 with the profile and golden vectors, or recording WP51 as owner.
